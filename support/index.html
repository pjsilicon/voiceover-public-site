Support

Need help with Voice Over AI Studio, subscriptions, account deletion, or App Review access?

Contact

Email: support@voice-over.app

For App Review demo-account support, include "Voice Over AI Studio App Review" in the subject line.

Account and Privacy Requests

Use Settings to check account deletion availability and start deletion when available. Deletion is supported for eligible accounts. Accounts with unresolved uploads or generation work, including legacy R2-backed uploads, may remain held and cannot complete deletion yet. For help with a deletion request or an earlier deletion awaiting recovery, email support from the account email address and include the request type in the subject line. Contacting support does not itself confirm that deletion is complete.

Sign-out and Reset sign-in do not delete an account or finish a pending deletion. They do not cancel an App Store subscription; manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Privacy Policy and account deletion details are available at /privacy#delete.

Purchases and Restore

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Use Restore Purchases in the app to resync entitlements. For refunds or billing changes, use Apple's purchase support flow from the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Media and Export Issues

When contacting support, include the approximate date of the issue, the workflow used, whether the clip was recorded or imported, and whether the issue happened before or after export. Do not include passwords or private account tokens.
